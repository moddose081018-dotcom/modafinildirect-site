Before you order

Is Modafinil Direct a legitimate vendor?

Modafinil Direct ships tracked parcels, publishes its delivery and refund terms and staffs a support desk. That does not remove the legal, quality or delivery risks of importing a prescription medicine, so verify the live terms yourself before paying.

Do you deliver to the UK?

Yes. Tracked delivery options for UK addresses are shown at checkout, along with the estimated timing and cost for each.

Is every delivery guaranteed?

No overseas delivery should be treated as guaranteed. Check the non-delivery deadline, reship remedy, refund rules and exclusions shown at checkout before paying.

What should I keep after ordering?

Save the order confirmation, the tracking number and a screenshot of the delivery and refund terms shown on the day you order. Support will ask for the order number if anything needs resolving.

Do I need a prescription?

Modafinil is a prescription-only medicine in the UK. Importing it for personal use is your decision and your responsibility. Speak with a UK clinician or registered pharmacist before using any medicine.

How do I contact support?

Through the contact page. Include your order number and the tracking number if your question is about a delivery.
