How ordering works

  1. Choose a product. Pick a brand and quantity from the catalogue.
  2. Check the terms. Confirm the delivery estimate, non-delivery remedy and total cost shown at checkout.
  3. Pay and keep your records. Save the order confirmation, tracking number and a screenshot of the terms on the day you order.
  4. Track your parcel. Follow the tracking link and contact support if anything stalls.

Before you order

Modafinil is a prescription-only medicine in the UK. Importing it for personal use carries legal, quality and delivery risks that no website can remove. Speak with a UK clinician or registered pharmacist about whether modafinil is appropriate for you, and read the current MHRA guidance on buying medicines online.

Modafinil Direct publishes its delivery and support terms so you can make an informed decision. If any detail cannot be confirmed, we say so rather than guess. Start with delivery, then the FAQ, and contact support with anything still unanswered.
