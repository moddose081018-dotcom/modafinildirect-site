About Modafinil Direct

What we do, what we stock, and what we will not promise.

What we do

Modafinil Direct supplies generic modafinil and armodafinil to customers who choose to import it for personal use. We keep the catalogue deliberately short, ship with tracking, and staff a support desk that answers questions about orders, deliveries and payments.

What we stock

Established generic brands from licensed manufacturers. Product listings show the manufacturer, strength and pack size so you can compare like with like. We do not list products we cannot source consistently.

What we will not promise

No overseas delivery is guaranteed. Customs inspections, carrier delays and address problems happen. What we do promise is that the delivery, reship and refund terms shown at checkout apply to your order, and that support will respond when you contact us.

Medical and legal position

Modafinil is a prescription-only medicine in the UK and in many other countries. We do not give medical advice, and nothing on this site should be read as an endorsement of using modafinil without clinical supervision. Speak with a qualified clinician or registered pharmacist before using any medicine.
