Delivery

Delivery, tracking and reship terms

How parcels are sent, how long they take, and what happens when one goes missing.

Delivery options

Every order ships with a tracking number. The carriers, estimated timings and costs available for your address are shown at checkout and are the ones that apply to your order. Save a screenshot of them on the day you order.

QuestionWhere to check
Which carriers deliver to my address?Checkout, after entering your address
How long will it take?The estimate shown at checkout for the option you pick
What does delivery cost?The order total before payment
Where is my parcel now?The tracking link in your dispatch email

Customs

Parcels entering the UK can be inspected. If a parcel is held or seized, contact support with your order number and any notice you received. The current non-delivery remedy shown at checkout tells you whether the order qualifies for a reship or refund and what deadline applies.

If a parcel does not arrive

  1. Check tracking first. Most delays resolve within the estimate shown at checkout.
  2. Contact support with your order number once the estimate has passed.
  3. Apply the published remedy. Reship or refund is handled under the terms in force when you ordered.

Delivery terms can change. The version shown at checkout when you pay is the one that applies to that order.
