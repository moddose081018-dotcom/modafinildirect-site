Delivery

Delivery, tracking and reship terms

How parcels are sent within the UK, how long they take, and what happens when one goes missing.

How we deliver

Every order is dispatched from within the UK to a UK address by Royal Mail tracked post. You receive a tracking number by email as soon as the parcel leaves us, and it arrives in a plain outer envelope with no product or brand information visible.

DetailWhat to expect
DispatchSame day for orders placed before 2pm, Monday to Friday; otherwise the next business morning
Delivery time1–3 business days for England, Scotland and Wales; Northern Ireland may take one day longer
TrackingRoyal Mail tracking number emailed at dispatch
PackagingPlain outer envelope, neutral return address
CoverageAny UK address Royal Mail delivers to, including PO boxes and lockers

No customs or import checks

Because every parcel is sent from inside the UK to a UK address, it is a domestic Royal Mail shipment. Nothing crosses a border, so there are no customs declarations, no import duties and no border inspections involved.

If a parcel does not arrive

  1. Check tracking first. Most delays resolve within a few days, particularly around bank holidays and peak periods. A tracking delay on its own does not mean the parcel is lost.
  2. Contact support with your order number once the estimated delivery date has passed. We will investigate with Royal Mail. Please report any delivery problem within 60 days of dispatch so we can act while tracking records are current.
  3. Lost parcel: one free replacement. We treat a parcel as lost when 10 working days have passed since its latest estimated delivery date, tracking shows no further movement, and Royal Mail has not confirmed delivery. At that point, or sooner if Royal Mail confirms the loss, we send one replacement at no additional cost on the same tracked service.
  4. Replacement also lost: full refund. If the free replacement is also confirmed lost, we refund the affected order in full, including the original delivery charge.

Other delivery problems

Parcels marked as delivered but not received, returned to us as uncollected, or sent to an address that was entered incorrectly are investigated individually with you and Royal Mail before a remedy is agreed. Where a parcel is returned to us uncollected, we can re-send it once it is back with us.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued within 10 business days of approval, or sooner where the law requires, to the original payment method where the provider supports it. How long the money then takes to appear depends on your bank or payment provider.

The 60-day reporting period is a request so we can investigate promptly. It does not limit your statutory rights, or any dispute rights you have through your payment provider.
