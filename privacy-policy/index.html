Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and for how long.

What we collect

To fulfil an order we collect your name, delivery address, email address and order details. Payment details are handled by the payment provider you choose at checkout and are not stored by us.

Why we collect it

To dispatch and track your order, to respond when you contact support, and to meet legal record-keeping obligations.

Who we share it with

Carriers, to deliver the parcel, and payment providers, to process the payment. We do not sell personal data.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as the law requires and then deleted. Support correspondence is kept for as long as the query is open and for a reasonable period afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we have no legal duty to keep it. Use the contact page.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses privacy-preserving, cookieless analytics to count visits. It does not use advertising cookies.
