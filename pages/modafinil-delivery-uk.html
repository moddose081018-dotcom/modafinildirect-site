How Long Does Modafinil Delivery Take When Ordering Online in the UK?

Standard delivery to any UK address takes 1–3 business days. Orders placed before 2pm Monday to Friday are dispatched the same day. You get a tracking number by email as soon as your parcel ships.

Quickest route: Order before 2pm on a weekday and your parcel leaves the warehouse the same afternoon. For most UK addresses, that means delivery the next business day.

Standard UK Delivery Times

Order time Dispatch Typical delivery
Before 2pm (Mon–Fri) Same day 1–2 business days
After 2pm (Mon–Fri) Next business morning 2–3 business days
Weekend Monday morning 2–3 business days

These timelines apply to mainland England, Scotland and Wales. Northern Ireland may take one additional day.

Same-Day Dispatch — Cutoff Time

The cutoff is 2pm, Monday to Friday. Orders submitted before this time go out the same afternoon. Orders placed after 2pm, or on weekends and bank holidays, are dispatched the next business morning.

You'll receive an email confirmation when your order is placed, and a second email with tracking details once it's dispatched. The tracking number works with Royal Mail's tracking portal.

Tracking and Packaging

Tracking: Every order ships with a tracked service. You'll get a Royal Mail tracking number by email within a few hours of dispatch. You can monitor progress at royalmail.com or via the Royal Mail app.

Packaging: Your order arrives in a plain outer envelope. There are no product names, no brand logos, and no indication of what's inside. The return address on the envelope is a neutral business address. This is standard on every order — you don't need to request it.

If Your Order Is Delayed

Royal Mail occasionally experiences delays, particularly around bank holidays and peak periods. If your tracking shows no update for more than 3 business days after dispatch, contact us with your order number. We'll look into it and, if the parcel is genuinely lost, arrange a replacement.

Note on customs: Orders are dispatched from within the UK — there are no customs or import declarations involved. This is a domestic UK shipment, not an international import.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is next-day delivery guaranteed?
We can't guarantee next-day delivery because Royal Mail's timelines vary. What we do guarantee: same-day dispatch on orders before 2pm, and a tracking number so you can monitor progress. Most orders in this category arrive the following business day.
Do you ship to PO boxes or lockers?
Yes. Any address Royal Mail delivers to, we can ship to.
What if I'm not home when it arrives?
Royal Mail will leave a card if you're not in and deliver to your local sorting office, or attempt re-delivery. You can also use Royal Mail's online service to redirect or reschedule delivery.
Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
Contact us as quickly as possible. If your order hasn't been dispatched yet, we can update the address. Once it's shipped, you'd need to use Royal Mail's redelivery service.
