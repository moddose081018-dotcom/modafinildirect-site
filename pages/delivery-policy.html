Delivery, Lost Parcel & Refund Policy

Every order ships from within the UK with Royal Mail tracking. Most parcels arrive in 1–3 business days. This page explains what happens on the rare occasions when one is delayed, lost, or not delivered — and what we'll do about it.

In short: if the carrier confirms your parcel is lost, we send one free replacement. If that replacement is also lost, we refund the order in full, including delivery. Report any problem within 60 days of dispatch so we can act quickly.

Delayed Deliveries

If your order passes its estimated delivery date, contact us with your order number and we'll investigate with Royal Mail. A tracking delay on its own doesn't mean the parcel is lost — most delayed parcels still arrive within a few days, particularly around bank holidays and peak periods.

We treat a parcel as lost when all three of the following apply:

  • 10 working days have passed since its latest estimated delivery date
  • Tracking shows no further movement
  • Royal Mail has not confirmed delivery

This matches Royal Mail's own threshold for a lost domestic parcel, and is the point at which we can raise a claim with them on your behalf.

Confirmed Lost Parcels

Once a parcel meets the lost criteria above, or Royal Mail confirms it as lost sooner, we will send one replacement at no additional cost. The replacement is dispatched on the same tracked service and you'll receive a new tracking number by email.

If the Replacement Is Also Lost

If the free replacement is also confirmed lost, we will refund the affected order in full, including the original delivery charge. We don't send a second replacement — at that point a refund is the fairer outcome for you.

Other Failed Deliveries

Some situations need looking at individually before we agree a remedy. We'll investigate each of these with you and Royal Mail:

Situation What we'll check
Tracking shows "delivered" but you haven't received it Royal Mail's GPS delivery scan and proof of delivery; whether it was left with a neighbour or in a safe place
Parcel returned to us as uncollected Whether a "something for you" card was left; we can re-send once the parcel is back with us
Sent to an incorrect address Whether the address on the order matches what you entered; whether the parcel can be recovered

Reporting a Delivery Problem

Please report any delivery problem as soon as possible, and preferably within 60 days of dispatch, so we can investigate while Royal Mail's tracking records are still current. Email us with your order number and a short description of the issue.

This requested reporting period does not limit your statutory rights, or any dispute rights you have through your payment provider.

Refunds

Approved refunds are issued within 10 business days of approval, or sooner where required by law. Refunds go to the original payment method where the provider supports it. How long the funds take to appear in your account after that depends on your bank or payment provider — typically 3–5 business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to wait the full 10 working days before contacting you?
No — contact us as soon as your parcel is overdue. We'll start investigating straight away. The 10-day threshold is when we can formally treat it as lost and dispatch a replacement, not when you're allowed to get in touch.
Can I choose a refund instead of a replacement?
Our standard remedy for a confirmed lost parcel is one free replacement. If you'd prefer a refund, tell us when you report the problem and we'll discuss it with you.
Are there any customs or import issues to worry about?
No. All orders are dispatched from within the UK to UK addresses. Your parcel is a domestic Royal Mail shipment — there are no customs declarations or import checks involved.
What if I gave the wrong address?
Contact us immediately. If the order hasn't been dispatched, we'll correct it. If it has, we'll try to recover the parcel through Royal Mail, but we can't guarantee a replacement or refund where the address you entered was incorrect.
