Why Generic Modafinil Costs Less Than Provigil

Provigil is the original brand-name version of modafinil, manufactured by Cephalon (now owned by Teva). A private prescription for Provigil in the UK typically runs to £200–£400 for 30 tablets — around £7–13 per tablet.

Generic modafinil contains the identical active ingredient at the same 200mg dose. The difference is the manufacturer. Companies like Sun Pharma and HAB Pharma produce generics under licence once the original patent expires. The drug itself is the same molecule.

The cost difference isn't about quality — it's about development costs. Cephalon spent decades and hundreds of millions developing and getting Provigil approved. Generics manufacture an already-proven compound without those R&D overheads.

Do Prices Drop for Larger Orders?

Yes, significantly. At 40 tablets, Modalert costs £2.20 per pill. At 300 tablets, that drops to £0.85 — less than half the price. If you're a regular user, larger packs make a real difference.
