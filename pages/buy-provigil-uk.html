Provigil isn't available without a prescription in the UK — but the generic versions are. Modalert, Modvigil and Modacare contain the exact same active ingredient (modafinil 200mg) at a fraction of Provigil's price. Here's what you need to know.

What Is Provigil?

Provigil is the original brand name for modafinil, developed by Cephalon (now a division of Teva Pharmaceuticals). It was first approved in the UK and US in the late 1990s for narcolepsy and later expanded to other sleep disorders. The Provigil brand name is what most people recognise — but the patent expired years ago, which opened the market to generics.

Provigil contains modafinil 200mg per tablet. That's the same dose, the same molecule, as every generic modafinil product.

Why Generic Modafinil Costs a Fraction of Provigil's Price

The price gap comes down to patent recovery. Cephalon spent decades and hundreds of millions developing Provigil, running trials, and getting it approved. They recovered those costs through exclusive pricing while the patent held.

Once the patent expired, companies like Sun Pharma could manufacture the same compound without those R&D overheads. The drug is identical. The cost structure isn't.

This is the same reason generic ibuprofen costs £1 at a supermarket while Nurofen costs £5. Same molecule, different packaging and brand.
